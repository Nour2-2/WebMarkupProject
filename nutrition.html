Nutrition & Vitamins

Healthy hair starts from within.

A balanced diet provides essential nutrients that support healthy hair growth and overall well-being.

Healthy Hair

Why Nutrition Matters

Hair needs nutrients to grow normally. Eating different types of healthy food helps your body get the nutrients it needs.

Protein, iron, zinc, and certain vitamins are important for normal hair health.

A balanced diet is usually a better starting point that taking supplements without knowing whether you need them.

Important Nutrients for Hair

Different nutrients help support your body's normal functions.

Examples of nutrients and food sources
Nutrients Why It Matters Food Sources
Protein Supports normal hair structure Eggs, fish, beans
Iron Supports normal hair maintenance Nuts, seeds, meat
Vitamin C Helps the body absorb plant-based iron Oranges, peppers, strawberries
Zinc Supports normal hair health Nuts, seeds, seafood

Simple Healthy Eating Habits

You do not need a complicated diet to support your overall health.

Small daily habits can help you maintain a balanced diet.

  1. Eat regular balanced meals.
  2. Include protein in your meals.
  3. Eat different fruits and vegetables.
  4. Drink enough water during the day.
  5. Ask a professional before taking supplements.

Healthy Hair Tip

Remember: Healthy hair depends on your overall health.

If you notice unusual or sudden hair loss, speak with a healthcare professional.
